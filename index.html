Classic Grilled Cheese

Prep Time: 5 minutes | Cooks in: 5 minutes

A quick, comforting, and perfectly crispy sandwich that takes minutes to make.

Ingredients

  • 2 slices of bread
  • 2 slices of Cheddar cheese
  • 1 tablespoon of butter

Directions

  1. Spread butter evenly on one side of each slice of bread.
  2. Place one slice, butter-side down, onto a hot skillet.
  3. Lay the cheese slices on top of the bread.
  4. Top with the second slice of bread, butter-side up.
  5. Grill until the bottom bread is golden brown and cheese starts to melt.
  6. Flip and grill the other side until golden. Serve hot!
